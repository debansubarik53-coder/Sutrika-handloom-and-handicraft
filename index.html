Our Mission

Preserving Odisha's Rich Cultural Heritage Through Digital Innovation

Odisha's handloom and handicraft traditions date back centuries, with techniques passed down through generations. From the intricate Ikat weaving of Sambalpur to the delicate silver filigree of Cuttack, each craft tells a story of cultural richness.

Sutrika bridges the gap between these timeless traditions and the modern world. By combining AI technology with artisan craftsmanship, we ensure these cultural treasures reach a global audience while supporting the livelihoods of artisan communities.
